# Example interpretation file: Decian libelli

This file meets every acceptance criterion. The question has one primary topical axis. Background knowledge fixes what a libellus is and when the persecution took place; the research checks it against the documents and decides how to recognize a libellus in the database. The research record in the papyrological-research skill and the good report in the report-writing skill were written from it.

---

# Question
How many Libelli from the persecution of Christians under Decius are preserved?

# Decisions
- Asked whether "preserved" means the libelli in the database or all published libelli. The user wants the database only.
- Final form: How many libelli from the persecution under Decius are preserved in the database?

# Terms
- Libelli: certificates stating that the holder had sacrificed to the gods before a local commission, as Decius' edict required. How one is recognized in the database (formula, address to the commission, signatures) is open; established by SQ1.
- Persecution of Christians under Decius: the general order to sacrifice issued by Decius around the turn of 249/250. The edict applied to all inhabitants, so a libellus alone does not show that its holder was Christian; tested by SQ3.
- Preserved: in the database (see Decisions); counted by SQ2.

# Scope
- Period: from Decius' order to sacrifice (around the turn of 249/250). The dates of the libelli themselves are not fixed in advance; they come out of SQ2.
- Region: open.
- Document types: libelli.
- Languages: open.
- Counts meant: database (SQ2).

# Topics involved
| Axis | Role | What the question asks along this axis |
|---|---|---|
| Temporal | not involved | The persecution identifies the documents; no change over time is asked. |
| Material | primary | Which documents are such libelli, and how many there are. |
| Relational | auxiliary | Whether the holders of the libelli can be identified as Christians. |
| Semantic | auxiliary | What a libellus of the persecution is, and by which wording it can be recognized. |

# Sub-questions
- SQ1 (semantic): By which features (formula, address, signatures) can a libellus of the Decian persecution be recognized in the database, and how are fragments and documents of unusual form decided?
- SQ2 (material): Which documents in the database are such libelli, and how many distinct papyri are they?
- SQ3 (relational): Do the libelli show whether their holders were Christians?

# Synthesis plan
SQ1 gives the criteria by which SQ2 identifies and counts the documents. SQ3 decides whether the libelli can be tied to Christians; if they can, it narrows the count of SQ2.
