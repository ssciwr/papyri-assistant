# Interpretation: Decian libelli

## Question
How many Libelli from the persecution of Christians under Decius are preserved?

## Restatement
How many libelli connected with the persecution of Christians under the emperor Decius are preserved?

## Terms
- Libelli: the kind of document the question asks about. The question does not say what kind of document it is, or how one can be recognized. Open; established by SQ1.
- Persecution of Christians under Decius: identifies which libelli are meant. Its date and what connects a document to it are open; established by SQ1.
- Preserved: see Readings.

## Scope
- Period: the persecution under Decius; its dates are open (SQ1).
- Region: open.
- Document types: libelli.
- Languages: open.
- Counts meant: open; see Readings.

## Readings
- "Preserved" as (a) in the database, or (b) in all published papyri: the question does not say which. Both are kept, as SQ2 and SQ3, and reported side by side.
- "Libelli from the persecution of Christians" as (a) libelli issued in the course of the persecution, or (b) libelli issued to Christians: the question does not settle which. Reading (a) is chosen as the main reading, because (b) can only be answered if the documents show the religion of their holders. SQ4 tests whether they do.

## Axes
| Axis | Role | What the question asks along this axis |
|---|---|---|
| Temporal | not involved | The persecution identifies the documents; no change over time is asked. |
| Material | primary | Which documents are such libelli, and how many there are. |
| Relational | auxiliary | Whether the holders of the libelli can be identified as Christians (reading b). |
| Semantic | auxiliary | What a libellus of the persecution is, and by which wording it can be recognized. |

## Sub-questions
- SQ1 (semantic, auxiliary): What kind of document is a libellus of the Decian persecution, when were such libelli issued, and by which features can one be recognized?
- SQ2 (material, primary): Which documents in the database are such libelli, and how many distinct papyri are they?
- SQ3 (material, primary): How many such libelli does the scholarly literature count, and how does that compare with SQ2?
- SQ4 (relational, auxiliary): Do the libelli show whether their holders were Christians?

## Synthesis plan
SQ1 gives the criteria by which SQ2 identifies the documents. SQ2 and SQ3 give two counts, which are reported side by side and not merged. SQ4 decides whether reading (b) can be answered; if it can, it narrows the count of SQ2.

## Expected answer
A count of preserved libelli with the full list and the criteria for inclusion, given separately for the database and for the literature.
