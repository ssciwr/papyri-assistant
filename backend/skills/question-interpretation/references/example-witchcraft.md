# Interpretation: witchcraft and religion

## Question
In what way is witchcraft attested in the papyri, and how does it relate to religion in pre-Christian vs Christian times?

## Restatement
How is witchcraft attested in the papyri, and how does its relation to religion differ between pre-Christian and Christian times?

## Terms
- Witchcraft: a modern term. The question does not say which ancient practices, terms or kinds of document it covers. Open; established by SQ1.
- Religion: the question does not say which religious practice the witchcraft is to be related to. Open; established by SQ5 for each period.
- Pre-Christian times, Christian times: the question divides time into two periods but does not say where the boundary lies, or whether a document belongs to a period by its date or by its content. Open; established by SQ2.

## Scope
- Period: all periods the papyri cover, divided into pre-Christian and Christian times (boundary open, SQ2).
- Region: open.
- Document types: open.
- Languages: open.
- Counts meant: not asked; counts may be needed to compare the periods.

## Readings
- "Attested" as (a) documents that are themselves acts of witchcraft (spells, amulets and the like), or (b) documents that mention witchcraft or accuse someone of it. The two show different things, the practice and how it was seen. The question does not exclude either, so both are kept, as separate parts of SQ3.

## Axes
| Axis | Role | What the question asks along this axis |
|---|---|---|
| Temporal | primary | How witchcraft and its relation to religion differ between pre-Christian and Christian times. |
| Material | auxiliary | Which documents attest witchcraft, and their type, date, language and place; their dates assign them to the periods. |
| Relational | primary | How witchcraft relates to religion: which powers the texts invoke, and how these relate to the religious practice of the same period; who wrote, commissioned or used the texts. |
| Semantic | primary | How witchcraft is expressed: terms, formulae, invocations, and what the documents that mention it call it. |

## Sub-questions
- SQ1 (semantic, auxiliary): Which practices, terms and kinds of document in the papyri correspond to what the question calls witchcraft?
- SQ2 (temporal, auxiliary): Where does the boundary between pre-Christian and Christian times lie in the papyri, and how can a document be assigned to one of the periods?
- SQ3 (material, auxiliary): Which documents attest witchcraft, (a) as acts of witchcraft or (b) by mentioning it, and how are they distributed by type, date, language and place?
- SQ4 (semantic, primary): Which terms, formulae and invocations do the documents use?
- SQ5 (relational, primary): Which powers do the documents invoke, and how do these relate to the religious practice of the same period? Who wrote, commissioned or used the documents?
- SQ6 (temporal, primary): How do the answers to SQ4 and SQ5 differ between pre-Christian and Christian times?

## Synthesis plan
SQ1 and SQ2 establish the category and the periods on which everything else depends. SQ3 gives the documents and their distribution over the periods, against which the comparison in SQ6 is measured. SQ4 and SQ5 describe witchcraft and its relation to religion within each period, and SQ6 compares the periods. The answer combines SQ4 and SQ5 into a description of how witchcraft is attested, and SQ6 into an account of how its relation to religion changed.

## Expected answer
A description of how witchcraft is attested, by kind of evidence, and an account of how its relation to religion differs between the two periods, supported by representative documents.
